PULSE

Daily ad-budget recommendations for the Meta and Google Ads accounts you own, inside the AI client you already use.

View on GitHub Quick start
PULSE never changes spend. There is no pause, mutate or apply tool. A proposal is a set of instructions you can carry out yourself in Ads Manager or Google Ads. Recording a move as “applied” records your report; it does not perform the action.

What you get

PULSE is an MCP server. It reads your campaigns, compares them and proposes where to scale or reduce daily budgets. Each move shows the observed window, conversions, CPA, ROAS, confidence and the reason, so your AI client can explain the numbers to you. PULSE itself makes no model calls.

  • Works with Claude Code and Claude Desktop (local stdio), and with Claude connectors, ChatGPT and Grok (remote Streamable HTTP, self-hosted).
  • Read-only grants: Meta ads_read; Google Ads restricted to tested read endpoints and fixed GAQL queries.
  • Credentials stay on your machine under ~/.config/pulse with owner-only permissions.
  • No Finsi account, database, warehouse or model-provider key is needed.

Why not just ask Claude to read the API?

You can. The difference is what decides the number. With PULSE, a tested, deterministic core computes every move; your AI client explains it. Asked freeform, the model computes the move itself, differently from one run to the next.

  • Same input, same answer. The recommendation core has no model calls and an explicit seed and clock, so a play can be re-run and audited.
  • A fixed, honest window. 30 days ending two days before today, so late-arriving conversions do not make yesterday look broken. Missing numbers stay missing; they never become zeros.
  • Moves are capped. Curve moves are damped by confidence and capped at 20%; proposals never exceed your total budget, and rounding never adds money.
  • It knows when not to move. Learning campaigns, shared or lifetime budgets, thin data and campaigns you protect are held, with the reason stated.
  • Money is exact. Each amount carries its currency, Meta budget offsets follow Meta's published table, and thresholds use your account currency.
  • Nothing can execute. There is no write tool. Proposals expire after 48 hours and are always applied: false.

One concrete case from the repository's synthetic fixtures: four accounts report the same 30-day ROAS for their two campaigns, 4.00 and 1.80. PULSE gives four different answers. On the stable account it moves +20% / −20%. With both campaigns in learning it holds both, because a bigger move would restart learning. On shared budgets it holds both, because moving one moves the pool. With only $30 of spend it holds both, because the move is under 1%. A budget split in proportion to ROAS would move about 38% each way in all four cases. The rules and their sources are in DESIGN.md.

We also ran freeform Claude on the same five fixtures, 10 runs each: 40 of its 100 budget decisions moved a budget by more than 20%, and one campaign got up to 4 different budgets for identical input. Benchmark method, results and caveats.

Try it with synthetic data

git clone https://github.com/finsilabs/pulse.git
cd pulse
npm ci
npm run build
node dist/index.js smoke --demo

Then connect your own accounts with node dist/index.js login meta or login google. See platform setup and the client install guides.

Tools

ToolWhat it returns
pulse.accountsAccounts your grant can see, with currency and timezone
pulse.snapshotValidated daily campaign metrics and observed budgets
pulse.todays_playScale, shift, hold, watching and warnings
pulse.explain_moveEvidence, window, target, cooldown and clamps for one campaign
pulse.propose_moveAn expiring action plan, always applied: false
pulse.configBounded targets, caps, protected campaigns and ceilings
pulse.record_decisionYour reported applied or dismissed decision, for cooldowns

What has been verified

The offline gate passes: rules and invariants, replayed synthetic Meta and Google responses, all seven tools, real stdio and Streamable HTTP transports, and OAuth with PKCE. A read-only live smoke against five real Meta ad accounts passed on 2026-11-06 and nothing was changed in them; it also caught and fixed a Meta pagination bug. Google Ads live checks and native-client screenshots are still to come. The verification record lists each check. These are test results, not a revenue-lift claim: validate every recommendation against your own business before you act on it.
